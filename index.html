05 / Good to know

A few answers
before we start.

Can you customize my existing Shopify store?

Yes. I can work on your current theme, homepage, collections and product pages. Share your store link and the changes you need on WhatsApp so we can agree on the scope.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your brand name, logo, product images and details, design preferences, and access to the relevant platform. We can confirm the complete list when discussing your project.

Does Rs. 2,500 include Shopify and a domain?

The starting price is for my store setup and customization service. Shopify subscription, domain registration, paid themes and paid apps are purchased separately.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the number of products, custom features and how ready your content is. I'll confirm an estimated delivery time after reviewing your requirements.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Responsive layouts are part of the service. I design navigation, sections and product pages to remain usable across mobile and desktop screens.
